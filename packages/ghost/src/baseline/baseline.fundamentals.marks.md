## Usage

Marks carry recognition through their approved shape and relationship to surrounding content. Their own supplied rules determine how they occupy the composition; a generic treatment cannot replace those decisions.

## Rules

- Use approved mark files and the supplied brand-specific clearspace and minimum-size rules; obtain missing source values rather than inventing universal dimensions.
- Place marks in visually clear areas and align them with related type or imagery when they share an anchor.
- Choose a permitted variant appropriate to the actual background so the complete mark remains recognizable.

## Never

- Never distort, redraw, recolor, outline, rotate, crop through, or reconfigure a mark unless the brand system explicitly permits that operation — instead use an approved asset and treatment.
- Never put a mark across abrupt color transitions or high-frequency imagery — instead use a clear region or controlled background.
- Never use a mark as decoration that competes with the primary message — instead give it its intended identity role.
