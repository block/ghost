description: Type settings fit the reading task and preserve detail.
severity: medium
references:
  - baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules
  - baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Never
---

- Flag line length falls outside the applicable documented range without an exemption, or repeated lines contain only one or two words. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag line spacing ignores the face, weight, language, width, or role, or the same role uses inconsistent line heights. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag a view exceeds the three-size or three-weight default without named recurring roles, or levels are indistinguishable at final size. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag role sizes drift across the artifact outside the opening-title exception, hierarchy jumps to the bottom of the scale rather than a step or two, or a title/subtitle pair relies only on line-height instead of an added spacing step. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag an embedded font lacks a font-face declaration covering a used weight. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag multiple emphasis devices compete, underlines collide with descenders, centered display text exceeds three lines, or justification produces rivers or extreme word spacing. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag paragraph spacing and indentation are both present, headings detach from their content, or line breaks split meaning, names, numbers and units, dates, or product names unnecessarily. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag hyphenation spans more than two consecutive lines or appears in names, URLs, or display copy; emphasized paragraphs end with one word; page breaks leave fewer than the documented two lines together. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag the rendered face lacks required characters or weights, software simulates styles, lines or accents collide, or fine details disappear at final size. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Rules`)
- Flag body paragraphs use manual breaks, sustained all caps, tight tracking, or decorative text effects in place of hierarchy. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Never`)
- Flag text is distorted or made too small to fit a container, or a one-off size emphasizes only a word or label. (`baseline.fundamentals.typesetting > Never`)

Review only applicable guidance. Do not apply a default flag where explicit brand or task guidance replaces that default.
