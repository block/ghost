- Flag claims, metrics, prices, specifications, scarcity, certification, or product behavior appear factual without evidence or qualification. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Rules`)
- Flag proposals, examples, placeholders, or unresolved facts appear approved, or conflicting credible sources are silently resolved for convenience. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Rules`)
- Flag fees, limits, risks, consent, or irreversible consequences are hidden before commitment, including missing applicable legal disclosures. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Rules`)
- Flag synthetic or placeholder media is presented as documentary proof, or fabricated media substitutes for unavailable authentic material. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Rules`)
- Flag a value presented as brand guidance has no supplied material or documented source; a comment claiming it is approved does not establish a source. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Rules`)
- Flag a missing-value report or unrequested brand rationale appears in rendered copy, or an illustrative-data label needed by the reader is hidden. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Rules`)
- Flag routine confirmations use disproportionate celebration or promotional language has no concrete relevant fact. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Rules`)
- Flag error copy blames the person, urgency is manufactured, or interchangeable praise replaces available evidence. (`baseline.fundamentals.content-integrity > Never`)

Review only applicable guidance. Do not apply a default flag where explicit brand or task guidance replaces that default; applicable legal disclosure requirements remain required.
