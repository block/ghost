- Flag one view contains five or more em dashes or three or more repeated aphoristic rebuttal cadences. (`baseline.fundamentals.model-tells > Rules`)
- Flag unsupported hover lift with growing shadow, generic accent colors, dark backgrounds, gradients, glass panels, or one-sided thick borders accumulate in the view. (`baseline.fundamentals.model-tells > Never`)
- Flag unrequested chat bubbles, repeated cards within cards, boilerplate headings, or automatic statistic templates replace content-specific choices. (`baseline.fundamentals.model-tells > Never`)
- Flag tracked uppercase kickers, isolated italic words, warm cream backgrounds, or whole-view centering appear without a documented reason. (`baseline.fundamentals.model-tells > Never`)
- Flag display statistics overwhelm operational content, decorative pills fill space, or sequence numbers imply an order that the content does not have. (`baseline.fundamentals.model-tells > Never`)

Review only applicable guidance. Do not apply a default flag where explicit brand or task guidance replaces that default.
Review frequency across the whole view, not one isolated choice; documented brand use is not a model tell.
