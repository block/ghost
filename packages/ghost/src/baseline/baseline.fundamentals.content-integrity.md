## Usage

Credibility depends on a reader being able to distinguish evidence from illustration. Representative content helps test a composition, but approval of a final design depends on its actual copy and assets. Legal content can need more space than promotional copy because completeness matters more than brevity.

## Rules

- Use final or realistically representative copy and imagery during development; evaluate the final composition with the actual required content rather than placeholders alone.
- Present claims, metrics, prices, specifications, availability, scarcity, certifications, source history, and product behavior as facts only when supported. Omit or qualify unsupported claims and visibly label illustrative or schematic data.
- Preserve names, numbers and units, dates, product names, and essential phrases when editing or breaking lines; keep grammar and meaning intact.
- When credible sources conflict, state the conflict or omit the disputed claim rather than selecting the convenient answer.
- Required: Include applicable legal, regulatory, disclosure, safety, licensing, and consent content accurately and completely. Reason: legal compliance; scope: content subject to those obligations and assets governed by their licenses.
- Use approved, properly licensed font and media files within their permitted uses.
- Let required legal copy exceed short-form copy limits while keeping its structure readable.
- Report missing brand values or authentic material to whoever directed the work; keep that report out of rendered copy unless the limitation is information the reader needs to judge the content.

## Never

- Never present synthetic, altered, or placeholder media as documentary proof of a real person, place, product, or event — instead label its illustrative status or use authentic evidence.
- Never supply a plausible brand fact to fill a gap — instead use a supplied authoritative material or report the unresolved value.
- Never edit away required qualifications to preserve a composition — instead make room for the complete information.
