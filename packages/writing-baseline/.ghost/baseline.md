This is a writing baseline, not a brand. It holds language decisions that are
right for any brand and names the decisions only a brand can make.

Authority runs in this order:

1. **Obligations** in `standard.language` hold everywhere. The brand controls
   how they sound, never whether they hold.
2. **Brand answers** hold next, once a human records them in a foundation
   chapter.
3. **Defaults** in `standard.copy-defaults` and chapter conventions hold until
   a brand answer replaces them.

Every foundation chapter marks its open questions as known gaps. Until a human
answers one, write plainly and label the choice provisional. Never present
your own choice as the brand's answer.

## If no guidance applies

Write plainly: lead with what matters, use the reader's words, and say what
happens next. Ask before choosing a name, a claim, or a tone the brand would
have to stand behind.
