for: Writing or editing any copy, or recording how a brand sounds.
---

Baseline chapter: every value is a known gap until a human answers it.

## Usage

Voice is the set of choices that makes copy sound like one brand rather than
any brand. It stays constant across moments; tone moves within it (see
`foundation.tone`). Voice works inside the obligations in `standard.language`,
never against them.

Record voice as rewrite pairs, not adjectives. "Friendly and bold" steers
nothing; a pair commits to a line:

- Generic: "Your payment was unsuccessful. Please try again."
- Brand: the line this brand would ship, written by the people who own the
  voice.

Collect pairs from those owners: lines they would ship, lines they would cut,
and the reason for each cut. Adjectives may label a set of pairs; they never
replace them. When answering a gap, restate the rule below as the brand's
current answer and keep at least one pair under it.

## Rules

- Known gap: warmth. How warm or cool does this brand sound at rest? Ask the
  human; do not invent an answer. Until answered, write plainly and label the
  tone provisional.
- Known gap: humor. Where is humor welcome, and where is it barred? Until
  answered, use none.
- Known gap: address. Does the brand speak as "we"? Does it address the reader
  as "you"? When, if ever, does "I" appear? Until answered, address the reader
  as "you" and use "we" only when the organization is taking responsibility.
- Known gap: register. How much specialist vocabulary does this brand's
  audience expect? Until answered, use everyday words and define the rest.
- Known gap: refusals. Which claims, words, or comparisons does this brand
  refuse that its competitors make? Until answered, apply
  `standard.copy-defaults` and add nothing of your own.
- Known gap: exemplars. Which shipped lines already sound right? Until a human
  supplies them, do not present any line as a voice example.

## Never

- Never record voice as adjectives alone; instead pair each adjective with a
  generic line and the brand's rewrite.
- Never fill a known gap with your own choice and present it as the brand's;
  instead write plainly and label it provisional.
- Never let voice override an obligation; instead keep the obligation and
  rewrite the words until both hold.
